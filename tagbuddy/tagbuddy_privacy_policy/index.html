TagBuddy Privacy Policy

This policy applies to all information collected or submitted on John Davis' website and TagBuddy for iPhone, iPad, and any other devices and platforms.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

TagBuddy does not collect personal information. Tag data that you create is stored only within the application's local sandbox on your device. TagBuddy does not upload this data to the internet or make it available to the developer.

GITHUB

This website is hosted by GitHub. GitHub has access to some basic technical information needed to perform this role, such as your IP address.

APPLE

Crash reports, logs, and rudimentary usage information may be collected by Apple and provided to us to help fix application crashes. This is controlled by your operating system settings and consent. Tag data stored by TagBuddy is not included or uploaded by the application.

ADS AND ANALYTICS

We do not add analytics frameworks or advertisements to TagBuddy.

INFORMATION USAGE

Because TagBuddy does not collect or receive your tag data, we do not use or share it.

SECURITY

Tag data is stored within TagBuddy's application sandbox and is protected by the standard security provided by your device and operating system. TagBuddy does not apply additional encryption to this data.

ACCESSING, CHANGING, OR DELETING INFORMATION

You can access, change, and remove your tag data through TagBuddy's main interface. Uninstalling the application also removes its locally stored data, subject to your device's operating system behavior.

CALIFORNIA ONLINE PRIVACY PROTECTION ACT COMPLIANCE

We comply with the California Online Privacy Protection Act. We therefore will not distribute your personal information to outside parties without your consent.

CHILDREN'S ONLINE PRIVACY PROTECTION ACT COMPLIANCE

YOUR CONSENT
